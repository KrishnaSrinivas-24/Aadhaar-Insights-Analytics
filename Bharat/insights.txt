Insight 1: State-wise Enrolment

Uttar Pradesh records the highest Aadhaar enrolments, followed by Bihar and Madhya Pradesh.

High enrolment numbers are concentrated in high-population states, indicating wider coverage and stronger enrolment demand.

Southern states such as Karnataka and Tamil Nadu also show consistent enrolment activity, reflecting steady participation across regions.

Insight 2: Age-group Analysis (Corrected)

The 0–5 age group shows the highest Aadhaar enrolments, indicating a strong focus on early-age registration.

The 5–17 age group also contributes significantly, likely driven by school-linked enrolment initiatives.

The 18+ age group has comparatively lower enrolments, suggesting that most adults are already enrolled and fewer new registrations are required.

Insight 3: Time-Trend Analysis (Fact-checked)

Aadhaar enrolment activity shows periodic fluctuations over time, with noticeable peaks during certain months.

These peaks may correspond to special enrolment drives or policy initiatives.

Despite fluctuations, enrolment continues throughout the year, indicating a stable and ongoing registration system rather than a declining trend.
