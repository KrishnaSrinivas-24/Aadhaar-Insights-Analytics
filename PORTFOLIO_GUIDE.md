## 🎓 Interview Questions You Might Get

### Technical Questions
**Q**: "How did you achieve 85% accuracy in forecasting?"
**A**: "We used weighted moving average (WMA) combined with exponential smoothing, with validation on historical patterns. We also implemented fallback mechanisms for edge cases."

**Q**: "How did you handle 5 million records?"
**A**: "Used pandas chunking for memory efficiency, implemented data cleaning pipelines, and optimized Plotly visualizations for performance."

**Q**: "What was the biggest challenge?"
**A**: "Identifying meaningful patterns across three different datasets (enrolment, biometric, demographic) that each had different temporal and demographic distributions."

### Behavioral Questions
**Q**: "Tell me about your team structure."
**A**: "We had 5 members: I handled architecture & integration, Bharat focused on enrolment analysis, Venkat on biometric patterns, Yashwanth on UI/UX, and Saravana on policy insights. This cross-functional approach was key."

**Q**: "How did you validate your results?"
**A**: "We used multiple validation approaches: cross-validation for ML models, domain expert review (Saravana), and comparison with government statistics."
